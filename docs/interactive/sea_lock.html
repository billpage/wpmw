The sea lock: phase field over phase space

Companion to ladder step 22, The sea as a phase reference.

Hue is the phase at every point of phase space: each point is traced back along its classical trajectory to t = 0, given the locked starting phase p0x0/ħ, and the Lagrangian action accumulated on the way is added. White curves are six sample rows of the sea, rippled by their own phase. Locked: every aligned pair samples its row's plane wave. Gas: every pair starts with a random phase.

What to look for. Along any one curve the phase stays continuous and satisfies dθ = p dx/ħ: a locked row is a Lagrangian manifold carrying the action. In phase space the field never folds. Folds appear only when a curve is seen along x, where two momenta sit over one place on two differently coloured patches — the loss of same-row coherence the step 22 probes measure.
