# docs/supplement

Extended analysis and discussion that supports, but is not part of, the
algorithm specifications in `docs/algorithm/`.

## Contents

- **`phase_space_crystal_lattice_supplement.md`** — Redrafted and restructured
  version of David Cyganski's project memo *Extended Fokker–Planck Eq. and
  the QLE V2*, focused on the phase-space crystal-lattice particle model and
  its stochastic microdynamics. Embeds key figures from his Sozi slide deck.
  Applies one sign correction to the V2 simplified-form discrete update; see
  §6.3 of the document.
- **`phase_space_crystal_lattice_4d_supplement.md`** — Companion document
  extending the 1+1-D microdynamics to the two smallest 4-D-phase-space
  cases: two particles in 1 spatial dimension, and one particle in 2 spatial
  dimensions. Derives both jump rules from the multi-dimensional QLE,
  compares them side by side, and shows that 2p/1D is "hidden 1+1-D" in
  centre-of-mass / relative-coordinate variables. Figures generated by
  `src/gen_microdynamics_4d_figures.py` and published to the `output`
  branch.
- **`phase_alignment_interaction_diagrams.md`** — Every elementary process of
  the phase-alignment layer drawn twice, in the format of §4 of the
  crystal-lattice supplement: once as a lattice picture in the `(x, p)` plane
  and once as a set of trajectories in the `(x, t)` plane. Covers the free
  leg, the pump, the two exchange directions `s = ±1`, and the suppressed
  channels, and argues in §6 that the list is complete. §0.1 records why the
  phrase "wave–particle interaction" does not survive into this layer — no
  quantum is absorbed or emitted, and every momentum-changing event is a
  two-body exchange between world-particles. §7 records a sign discrepancy
  exposed by drawing the pump: the kick sign of §6 of the algorithm
  specification and the quadrature of Lemma 5 differ by one factor of −1, and
  the likeliest home for it is the unresolved direction label `s`. Figures
  generated by `src/gen_phase_alignment_interaction_diagrams.py` and published
  to the `output` branch.
- **`four_action_foundations.md`** — Reading of Cyganski's note *A journey from
  Bohm trajectory theory, through Nelson's SDEs and Wigner Particles to the
  Closed Four Action Model* (3 August 2026) against the derivation ladder in
  `../analysis/`, focused on the note's closing question: do momentum and
  energy balance determine the four-action rates? Page 4 of the note closes
  open item 1 of `four_rule_microdynamics_equivalence.md` — the reconstructed
  hop rate matches exactly. The answer to the closing question is no:
  Proposition F1 shows conservation is *implied* by QLE-exactness for every
  member of the rate family, and Proposition F2 shows the focus gain is
  invisible to moments 0–2 and first appears at moment 3, because it multiplies
  the first quantum correction. Theorem F3 identifies the true selection
  principle as endpoint locality — Cyganski's own boxed remark that the
  intermediate-state density no longer weights the actions — which selects the
  model uniquely on its own. Proposition F4 isolates the residual quantum input
  to a single postulate: the momentum grid step is half the photon momentum of
  the mode; below it the model is classical Liouville, away from it Moyal at
  the wrong Planck constant. Figures generated by
  `src/demo_four_action_foundations.py` and published to the `output` branch.
- **`figures/`** — Figures cited by
  `phase_space_crystal_lattice_supplement.md`, reproduced from Cyganski's
  *Wigner Collisions Diagram* Sozi presentation. Figures *generated* by
  project scripts are not committed here; they live on the orphan `output`
  branch and are embedded via
  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/billpage/wpmw/output/figures/<filename>`.
