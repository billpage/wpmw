My position, in its strongest form, is this. In step 25 we found that our one-particle vocabulary had quietly defined a world-particle to be a world. I also have a correction to my own framing, which comes on slide 13. Much of what feels like the many-particle problem is really a many-dimension problem. What the one-particle ladder hid was mostly that it worked in one dimension, with external potentials and no bonds, rather than the single particle as such.
